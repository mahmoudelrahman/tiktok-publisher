Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how TikTok Claude MCP ("the App") handles your information.

1. Information we access

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), through the user.info.basic scope. Used to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok after you approve the App. Used to upload and publish videos for you.
  • Videos and captions that you choose to publish through the App.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to upload, publish and schedule content to your own TikTok account at your request. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens and scheduled-post details are stored locally on the App owner's device. They are not stored on third-party servers. Videos are sent directly to TikTok.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Content is only sent to TikTok, through its official API.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the App or revoke access. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Locally stored tokens are deleted when you disconnect.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page.
