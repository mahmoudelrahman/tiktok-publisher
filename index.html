Coupona Publisher

Coupona Publisher is the in-house publishing tool of Coupona (coupona.shop), a website of discount codes for online stores. It lets the Coupona team post and schedule videos and photo posts to Coupona's own TikTok account, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. Connect the TikTok account with "Continue with TikTok" (TikTok Login Kit).
  2. Choose a video or photos, write the caption, and choose who can see the post, interaction settings and content disclosure.
  3. Post now, or schedule the post for a later time.

The tool is used only for Coupona's own account. It is not offered to other users.
